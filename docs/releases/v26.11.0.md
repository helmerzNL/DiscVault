# v26.11.0 — TV series from MovieVault, end to end

_Released: 2026-11-03_

## Highlights

This release makes a television release behave like one all the way through. A TV
disc added from MovieVault — by barcode, from the "Which pressing is this?" list or
from a title search — is now added as a **series**, linked to its seasons, and the
next release of the same show joins the same series. A series is found by **any of
its ids** (TMDB television id or TheTVDB id), so the same show arriving through two
providers no longer becomes two series. A confirmed show is described from TMDB's
**television** namespace instead of borrowing the synopsis, cast and artwork of an
unrelated film with the same number. And the season links you curate here can be
**sent back to MovieVault** as corrections, per release or for a whole series at once.

Alongside that, a collection no longer vanishes when you file a TV show into it, a box
set nested in a collection shows a cover, the Seasons tab no longer fails when a source
leaves an episode field empty, and 105 labels that were English in every language are
now translated.

**Read the upgrade notes before deploying** — there are no database migrations, but two
bundled plugins move up (MovieVault v2 to 1.12.0, TMDb to 1.11.0) and the series work
depends on the installed copies actually being upgraded.

## ✨ Features

- **Find a series by any of its ids, and keep TheTVDB's id on it**
  ([#833](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/833),
  [#826](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/issues/826)). A series could only be
  found by the one id it was created under, so a show picked from TheTVDB and the same
  show arriving from MovieVault became two series, and a TV work known only by its
  TheTVDB id linked to nothing. A series block now carries every id it states
  (`tmdb_tv`, `tvdb`); a series is found by any of them, `tmdb_tv` first, and gains the
  ids it lacks — fill-only, refusing an id another series already holds. MovieVault v2
  1.12.0 maps `tvdbId` from the resolver, and TMDb 1.11.0 asks for `external_ids` on
  `series_details`, so a series refresh files TMDB's TheTVDB id on a series that has
  none. A disc on a series none of the stated ids names no longer mints an empty series
  on every refresh. The rules are recorded in App-Guidance,
  `media-type-and-series-hierarchy` §7r.

- **Send a TV release's season links to MovieVault**
  ([#836](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/836)). Which seasons a TV release
  covers is now a MovieVault field correction, `seasons` (MovieVault-v2 0.44.0), sent as
  the sorted season numbers the release is linked to here (`0` for the specials).
  - **Per release:** the correction sheet offers `seasons` like any other field. A
    release linked to no season proposes nothing — upstream reads `[]` as "unlink every
    season" — and a work MovieVault holds as a film is withheld as
    `catalogue_records_a_film` instead of being sent to a refusal.
  - **Per series:** the series screen's Seasons panel gains **Send season links to
    MovieVault**. It compares every release of the series with the catalogue and sends
    each ticked one as its own seasons-only correction, so a moderator judges each box
    on its own. New routes: `GET /api/next/movievault/contributions/series-seasons` and
    `POST /api/next/movievault/contributions/series-seasons/submit`.

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- **A scanned TV release got no series, and the next release of the show got another
  one** ([#830](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/830),
  [#826](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/issues/826)). Three causes, which only
  together make the link work — and safe:
  1. The `series` block the MovieVault plugin has always emitted never reached the
     merge, because `canonicalize_plugin_result` did not carry it across. It now does,
     for MovieVault only: series identity is identity, which MovieVault owns.
  2. A statement without seasons would then have erased the owner's: the feed omits
     `seasons` when nobody curated them, which was read as `[]` and pruned every season
     and episode link on the disc. An empty statement now leaves the links alone; a
     non-empty one still replaces them.
  3. The same show under two providers was two series, because the lookup matched on
     the provider as well as the id. It now matches on the namespace and the id; the
     oldest live series wins if duplicates already exist.

- **A TV disc stayed a film although MovieVault knew its series and seasons**
  ([#831](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/831)). A sync only ever updated
  the local index, so a disc scanned shortly before MovieVault reclassified its release
  stayed a film. The sync now reports which releases changed what they say about their
  work — work type, TMDB television id, TheTVDB id, seasons — and the worker queues an
  ordinary metadata refresh for every live disc carrying one of them (once per disc
  while one is pending, at most 500 per sync, logged when capped). A refresh with
  nothing else to change no longer skips the series link, and the live release-details
  fallback may now carry seasons as well. MovieVault v2 1.11.0.

- **A TV pressing picked from a list or a title search was added as a film**
  ([#832](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/832)). The "Which pressing is
  this?" path applied only the edition's own facts, and the title-search path stripped
  the `mediaType` and `series` MovieVault sends. Both now give the import the type and
  the series block a synced record carries, linked through the same
  `apply_movie_series_link`. A type or series already in the proposal still wins.

- **A MovieVault series answer was refused outright**
  ([#828](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/828)). MovieVault 0.40.0 reports a
  catalogued series by its TMDB television id with a `/tv/` link, and the
  release-details reader — which derived the expected link from the film id alone —
  rejected the whole answer as `release_details_response_invalid`. It now accepts
  `tmdbTvId` beside `tmdbMovieId` (refusing the two together), derives the expected link
  from whichever is present, and keeps a stated `workType`. MovieVault v2 1.10.0 carries
  `tmdbTvId` as `providerIds.tmdb_tv`, never `tmdb`.

- **A confirmed show was described with a film's metadata**
  ([#827](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/827),
  [#829](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/829)). *Band of Brothers* showed
  the synopsis, cast and artwork of the film *Scorched* (TMDB 4613 is both, in different
  namespaces), and the 2006 *Planet Earth* those of the unrelated 1974 film via a title
  fallback. A stated SHOW now withholds TMDB's film namespace from the refresh (#827),
  and TMDb 1.10.0 adds `show_details`, the television sibling of `movie_details`,
  answering from `/tv/{id}` in the shape the title merge already reads (#829). Identity
  comes only from an id — the series' `tmdb_tv`, a `tmdbTvId`, or an IMDb id resolved
  through `/find` against `tv_results` only — never from the film `tmdbId` or a title.
  Cast and crew come from `aggregate_credits` (the whole run); television-only genres
  are mapped onto the film catalogue where one exists. An instance still on TMDb 1.9.0
  keeps the withheld state and gets no title metadata for a confirmed show.

- **MovieVault without a merge operation looked like a retryable refusal**
  ([#834](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/834),
  [#699](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/issues/699)). An older MovieVault
  answers the couple-and-merge call with 404 or 405 and no contract error code. That
  case is now reported as `merge_unsupported` — terminal, not retryable — so a client
  can stop offering the merge. Both identities stay intact, as before.

- **The Seasons tab failed when a source left an episode field empty**
  ([#837](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/837)). TheTVDB has no still or
  summary for an episode that has not aired, TMDB no air date for one only announced;
  one such episode failed the whole refresh with `'NoneType' object is not
  subscriptable` and nothing was stored. An empty field is now stored as NULL.

- **A collection vanished when a TV show was filed into it**
  ([#824](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/824),
  [#823](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/issues/823)). The series tile claimed
  every disc it groups before the containers did, so a collection holding only a show's
  discs was left empty and dropped. A collection now claims its discs first; against a
  box set or a vault the series still wins, unchanged.

- **A box set nested in a collection still showed no cover**
  ([#820](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/820),
  [#811](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/issues/811)). Most box sets own no
  artwork and borrow a member's poster everywhere else; the collection items view now
  borrows it too, through the same `containerTilePosterUrl` resolver.

- **105 labels were English in every language**
  ([#821](https://github.com/helmerzNL/DiscVault/pull/821)). Keys used by
  `next_views_ui.py` (admin, import centre, person and title detail, profile, sign-in,
  bulk actions) existed in no catalogue and fell back to inline English. All 29 locales
  now carry them; the English UI is unchanged.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

**No database migrations.** This release adds no migration; a backup before upgrading
is still good practice, as always.

**Check the plugins actually upgraded.** Plugins run from the writable install directory
and are only replaced when the bundled copy is strictly newer. This release needs
**MovieVault v2 1.12.0** and **TMDb 1.11.0**; **Admin → Plugins** shows the installed
versions. An instance left on TMDb 1.9.0 gets no title metadata for a confirmed show
(the refresh reports `series_identity` and stops), and one left on an older MovieVault v2
does not link TV discs to their series.

**Existing TV discs are linked gradually, not all at once.** After the upgrade, a MovieVault
sync queues a metadata refresh only for discs whose release changed what it says about its
work (type, TMDB television id, TheTVDB id, seasons) — at most 500 per sync. The very first
full snapshot after a fresh index reports nothing. A disc that is already a SHOW without a
series is linked on its next metadata refresh; a TV disc that should be a series and is
not can be refreshed by hand. A disc whose type is locked to MOVIE is deliberately left
alone.

**Records already contaminated by a film's metadata are not cleaned up.** A show that was
given a film's synopsis or cast before this release (the *Band of Brothers* / *Scorched*
case) has its empty fields filled and the fields a TMDB answer always replaces (rating,
genres, origin, unchosen artwork) corrected on its next refresh, but a non-empty overview
and credits a film added stay until they are edited by hand.

**MovieVault compatibility.** Nothing here requires a newer MovieVault to keep working.
Series answers are read from MovieVault 0.40.0 onwards, pressing seasons from 0.41.0, and
the season-link correction needs MovieVault-v2 0.44.0. Against a MovieVault that has no
merge operation, the merge now reports `merge_unsupported` instead of a generic error.

**No manual reload is required.** No preference is overridden, and nothing that was on is
turned off.

### Deployment files

- **No deployment-file changes in this release.** No Compose file and no `.env.example`
  changed, and no environment variable was added, renamed or removed: an existing
  deployment keeps working untouched.
- **No size, rate or timeout limit changed in this release**, so nothing running in front of
  DiscVault — a reverse proxy, an ingress, a CDN — needs adjusting. The two new routes are
  ordinary `GET`/`POST` requests under `/api/next/movievault/contributions/`.
